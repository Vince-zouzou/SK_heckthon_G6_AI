How PharmIQ works

Four specialised agents orchestrated by an executive AI.

01

Criteria Extraction

Reads your dossier and derives search criteria — indication, MoA, comparators, line of therapy, biomarkers — to query NICE.

02

Analog Benchmarking

Ranked analogs by similarity and disease relevance, with NICE TA / HST source links, ICERs and decisions.

03

HTA Prediction

Accept / Reject probability with rationale plus a list of evidence to add to flip the verdict in your favour.

04

Price Corridor

Recommended price range anchored on analog ICERs, willingness-to-pay thresholds, and value drivers.
